# Singapore National AI Strategy 2.0 (NAIS 2.0)
**Author:** Smart Nation and Digital Government Office (SNDGO) & IMDA
**Publication Date:** December 2023
**Classification:** Open / Public Policy Framework
**Doc ID:** SG-NAIS-2023

## 1. Vision and Strategic Shift
Singapore launched National AI Strategy 2.0 (NAIS 2.0) under the mantle "AI for the Public Good, for Singapore and the World". Whereas NAIS 1.0 (2019) focused on nine targeted lighthouse projects, NAIS 2.0 elevates AI to a pervasive national capability. The strategic intent is to foster widespread mastery, high-impact societal deployment, and trusted global leadership in AI governance and assurance.

## 2. Anchor Pillar 1: Talent Pipeline Expansion
Singapore has committed to tripling its pool of world-class AI practitioners and data scientists to 15,000 by 2028 (up from approximately 5,000 in 2021). Key initiatives include:
- The AI Visiting Professorship and National AI Fellowships to attract global AI researchers.
- Expanded AI Apprenticeship Programmes (AIAP) administered in partnership with AI Singapore, scaling cohort intake by 150%.
- Mid-career TechSkills Accelerator (TeSA) conversion tracks prioritizing software engineers transitioning into specialized machine learning, evaluation, and LLM fine-tuning roles.

## 3. Anchor Pillar 2: Compute and Cloud Infrastructure
Recognizing compute capacity as a critical bottleneck for sovereign AI development, the Government has earmarked SGD 500 million over five years for high-performance compute access. This fund subsidizes GPU clusters and high-bandwidth interconnects for local universities, research institutes, and indigenous AI startups, alongside designated sovereign cloud tenants for sensitive public administration workloads.

## 4. Anchor Pillar 3: High-Impact Sectoral Transformations
NAIS 2.0 prioritizes three anchor sectoral deployments:
1. **Public Health and Preventive Care:** Deploying clinical LLMs for clinician note synthesis, medical imaging triage, and population risk stratification under Healthier SG.
2. **Public Sector Productivity:** Deploying Pair (a secure internal LLM assistant) to over 140,000 civil servants to accelerate policy research, document drafting, and data synthesis.
3. **Advanced Manufacturing & Green Logistics:** Utilizing reinforcement learning and generative simulation for maritime slot optimization and semiconductor defect detection.

## 5. Anchor Pillar 4: Trust, Safety, and Model Governance
Singapore will not regulate AI using blunt pre-market licensing. Instead, Singapore enforces practical, principles-driven frameworks:
- **AI Verify Foundation:** Global open-source testing toolkit and governance testing framework for testing bias, robustness, and hallucination.
- **Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI (2024):** Articulating nine operational dimensions, including accountability, data provenance, model alignment, red teaming, and incident reporting.
